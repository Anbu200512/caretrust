Zero Downtime Guarantee

Life Support Redundancy: Prepared for Any Blackout

When a patient relies on mechanical ventilation or supplemental oxygen, power failures are life-threatening. We install a military-grade dual redundancy safety net in every home.

Medical-Grade Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)

Instant battery switchover in 8 milliseconds. Keeps ventilators humming without a microsecond of interruption.

Standby High-Pressure Emergency Oxygen Tanks

Every concentrator rental includes two non-electric compressed gas E-tanks with regulators for severe storm emergencies.

Priority Utility Grid Registration

We register your home with the local electric utility company as a Priority Life Support Residence for fast grid restoration.

Severe Weather Warning Deployment
