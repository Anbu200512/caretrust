How CareTrust Launches Your Care Plan

From the first exploratory call to ongoing bedside delivery, every phase is coordinated with hospital precision.

1

Clinical Discovery Call

A 15-minute phone consultation with a registered nurse triage officer to identify diagnoses, mobility constraints, medical equipment, and family preferences.

Turnaround: 15 - 30 minutes
2

In-Home Clinical Audit

A Clinical Nursing Supervisor visits your residence to conduct a 40-point environmental safety check, vital assessment, medication review, and fall risk score.

100% Free & No-Obligation
3

Clinician Match & Plan

We pair your family with certified caregivers or nurses who match clinical expertise, personality, and language preference. Family reviews bios beforehand.

100% Match Guarantee
4

Active Care & Telemetry

Care commences with supervisor orientation. Real-time shift notes, vitals logging, and medication administration sync live to your Family Portal.

24/7 Digital Visibility

Intake Preparation

What to Prepare for Your Assessment

To make your free in-home clinical audit as productive as possible, having these simple items ready helps our RN supervisor tailor medication and care routines instantly.

Don't have everything handy?

No problem! Our case coordinators will contact your physician's office directly to obtain hospital discharge papers and medical history with your consent.

1

Medication Bottles & Schedule

Current prescription bottles, over-the-counter vitamins, and dosages for clinical reconciliation.

2

Discharge Summary / Diagnosis

Hospital or rehab discharge instructions detailing surgical precautions, diet, and activity restrictions.

3

Insurance & ID Cards

Medicare Part A/B card, Medicare Advantage, private insurance, or Long-Term Care Insurance policy number.

4

Emergency Contacts & Wishes

Key family proxies, healthcare power of attorney documents, and preferred emergency hospital destination.
