Infection Prevention

Sterile In-Home Surgical Field Standards

We recreate hospital operating room sterile discipline directly inside the patient bedroom.

1. Dedicated Clean Zone Setup

Establishment of a designated medical staging area with antimicrobial barriers, dedicated sharps container, and biohazard disposal.

2. Single-Use Sterile Consumables

100% factory-sealed surgical gloves, chlorhexidine skin preps, sterile drapes, and vacuum drainage canisters never reused.

3. Double-Check Verification

High-risk infusions (insulin, heparin, intravenous potassium) require secondary barcode digital verification before infusion.

Emergency Protocols

24/7 Clinical Triage Escalation Pathway

What happens when vital signs fluctuate or an emergency occurs at 2:00 AM.

Step 1

Automated Vital Threshold Flag

Telemetry device flags abnormal SpO2 (<90%), BP spike, or irregular heart rhythm immediately.

Step 2

Bedside RN Assessment

On-duty nurse checks airway, auscultates lung sounds, reviews medication timing, and stabilizes vitals.

Step 3

Attending MD Teleconference

Direct secure video line to the on-call physician for immediate verbal orders and medication adjustments.

Step 4

Rapid Mobile Response Dispatch

Clinical supervisor or mobile respiratory equipment dispatched to home if condition requires supplemental support.

Clinical Outcomes

Evidence-Based Wound Healing Milestones

Documenting epithelialization progress with calibrated digital planimetry measurements.

Week 1–2

Exudate Control & Debridement

Negative pressure vacuum applied, necrotic tissue removed, sterile wound borders sealed, pain managed.

Week 3–5

Granulation Tissue Proliferation

Healthy pink vascular tissue builds in wound bed, depth decreases by 40–60%, high-protein enteral intake tracked.

Week 6–8

Epithelial Closure & Scar Maturation

Full margin closure, skin barrier creams applied, transition off vacuum dressing to protective hydrocolloids.
