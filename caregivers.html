Safety Guarantee

Our 5-Tier Caregiver Screening Shield

We only send individuals into your home whom we would trust with our own parents.

Tier 1: FBI Fingerprints

Multi-state criminal records and sex offender registry clearances.

Tier 2: 10-Panel Drug Screen

Pre-employment and randomized drug screening protocols.

Tier 3: CNA / HHA Verification

Verification of state nursing aide licenses and active standing.

Tier 4: Hands-On Transfer Test

Physical demonstration of gait belt and slide-board transfers.

Tier 5: Empathy Interview

Behavioral assessment evaluated by Clinical Nurse Directors.

Memory Care Methodology

Gentle Sensory Engagement to Reduce Anxiety & Sundowning

When memory fades, emotions and sensory memories remain vibrant. Our caregivers utilize music from the patient's youth, tactile aromatherapy, and calm redirection techniques rather than pharmacological sedation.

Personalized historic playlist therapy
Tactile memory boxes with vintage fabrics and keepsakes
Circadian phototherapy to reset sleep cycles
