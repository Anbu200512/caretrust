Portal Architecture

Three Dedicated Healthcare Portals

Family & Patient Portal

Access live vitals telemetry, read nurse shift handover notes, track recovery milestones, and communicate with your case manager.

Field Clinician Station

Registered Nurses and therapists submit eMAR medication records, log wound photographic progression, and verify shift clock-ins via GPS EVV.

Hospital Discharge Planners

Inpatient case managers upload acute discharge facesheets, sign Medicare Plan of Care forms, and confirm same-day hospital step-downs.

Security Rigor

Protecting Sensitive Health Information

Under Federal HIPAA regulations, patient biometric data and medical records require strict electronic safeguards. Our portal implements mandatory multi-factor authentication (MFA) and automatic session termination after 15 minutes of inactivity.

Biometric Passkey Support

Sign in with Apple FaceID, TouchID, or Windows Hello on compatible devices.

Encrypted Audit Logs

Every record view and export is permanently logged with timestamp and user ID.

Your Privacy Rights Guarantee

CareTrust never sells, monetizes, or shares patient health data with commercial advertisers. Access is strictly granted to designated family proxies and verified clinical care team members.

Frequently Asked Portal Access Questions

How can an adult child obtain access to an aging parent's portal?

The patient or legal healthcare power of attorney (DPOA) can add designated family proxies during intake registration or by calling their care coordinator.

What should I do if my one-time registration link expired?

Registration links expire after 48 hours for security reasons. Click 'Forgot Password' above to receive an instant re-activation email.
