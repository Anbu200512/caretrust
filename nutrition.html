Dietetic Methodology

Our 4-Phase Nutrition Protocol

Evidence-based clinical calculations ensure adequate caloric, protein, and micronutrient intake.

1

Metabolic Baseline Audit

Review of serum albumin, prealbumin, BUN/Creatinine, BMI, and stage of pressure injuries or surgical incisions.

2

Formula Prescription

Selection of specialized clinical formulas (e.g. Jevity, Peptamen, Nepro, Promote) with specific protein-to-calorie ratios.

3

Caregiver Demonstration

Registered nurse trains family members on pump priming, 30-degree head elevation rules, and residual gastric volume checks.

4

Bi-Weekly Weight Scans

Dietitian reviews weekly weight logs, skin turgor, wound granulation, and adjusts caloric density to prevent muscle catabolism.

Swallowing Safety

Eliminating Aspiration Pneumonia in Stroke & Dementia

Aspiration pneumonia is one of the leading causes of preventable hospital re-admissions among elderly patients. Our team integrates certified speech pathologists and dietitians to ensure 100% compliant food textures.

International Dysphagia Diet (IDDSI) Testing

Fork drip and spoon tilt tests conducted at every meal to ensure exact viscosity specifications (Levels 3 through 7).

Post-Prandial Upright Positioning Protocol

Caregivers maintain patient in 90-degree upright posture during meals and 45-degree angle for at least 45 minutes post-feeding.

IDDSI Texture Framework

Level 4: Pureed / Extremely Thick Smooth, no chewing needed
Level 5: Minced & Moist 4mm particle limit
Level 6: Soft & Bite-Sized 15mm particle limit
Level 2: Mildly Thick (Nectar) Flows through prong
