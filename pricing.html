Common Billing & Reimbursement Questions

When does Medicare cover home health care?

Medicare covers 100% of skilled nursing and physical therapy visits when a patient is considered "homebound" (requiring considerable effort to leave home) and care is ordered by an authorized medical provider. Companion and custodial care are typically private pay or LTCI.

How does direct Long-Term Care Insurance billing work?

With your signed Assignment of Benefits (AOB), CareTrust files the daily clinical care notes, nurse timesheets, and invoices directly with your insurer so you don't have to front large out-of-pocket payments.

Are there extra fees for medication setup or supervisor visits?

No. Registered Nurse supervisory evaluations, digital Family Portal software access, and caregiver matching are completely included in the published hourly rate.
