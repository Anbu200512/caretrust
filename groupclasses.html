No Sitter Needed

Our Free On-Site Clinical Respite Promise

The #1 reason family caregivers miss support groups is because they cannot leave their loved one alone at home. When you attend any in-person CareTrust class, you bring your parent or spouse with you.

In our adjacent sensory quiet room, licensed Certified Nursing Assistants provide continuous companionship, gentle music therapy, cognitive games, and light refreshments—all 100% free of charge.

Respite Suite Features:
Licensed CNAs with Dementia Credential
Calming acoustic & lighting environment
Assistance with restroom & mobility
Immediate proximity to class room
Practical Skills

Core Clinical Competencies Mastered

We replace anxiety with concrete clinical techniques you can use the moment you return home.

1

Vitals & Sepsis Recognition

Detect early fever, sudden confusion, or rapid pulse before a minor UTI turns into life-threatening sepsis.

2

Spine-Safe Transfers

Body mechanics, center-of-gravity principles, and assistive gait belt handling to protect your back.

3

Dementia Validation Method

Never argue with dementia reality. Learn Naomi Feil's validation therapy to de-escalate anxiety peacefully.

4

Medication Regimen Setup

Color-coded blister packs, crushable vs non-crushable pills, and coordinating with specialty mail-order pharmacies.
