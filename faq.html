Category I

Clinical & Nursing Protocols

Questions regarding medical procedures, licensing, prescription fulfillment, and physician communication.

What credentials and licenses do CareTrust nurses hold?

Every CareTrust nurse is either a Registered Nurse (RN) holding a Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) or a Licensed Practical Nurse (LPN) in good standing with state licensing boards. All clinical staff hold current Basic Life Support (BLS) and Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS) certifications and have at least 3 years of acute inpatient hospital experience.

Do I need a doctor's prescription before receiving home care?

For non-medical companion and personal care (Tier I), no doctor's prescription is required—you can start care immediately. For skilled nursing (Tier II) and high-acuity ventilator management (Tier III) requiring medical administration or Medicare reimbursement, a physician's order is needed. Our intake team can contact your physician's office directly to obtain and coordinate this.

How are IV antibiotics, PICC lines, and total parenteral nutrition (TPN) managed at home?

Our IV certified Registered Nurses follow hospital-grade sterile techniques for dressing changes, flushing, and heparin locking. We coordinate directly with home infusion specialty pharmacies to ensure delivery of sterile compounded medications, infusion pumps, and tubing directly to your residence.

How does CareTrust coordinate with our family doctor or surgeon?

Our digital electronic health record (EHR) transmits weekly clinical summaries, wound healing photographic progressions, and vital trends directly to your physician's medical office via secure HIPAA electronic facsimile or direct EMR integration. If vital signs deviate from prescribed parameters, the on-call physician is alerted immediately.

Can CareTrust provide physical and occupational therapy alongside nursing?

Yes. Our multidisciplinary care team includes Doctorate-level Physical Therapists (DPT), Occupational Therapists (OTR/L), and Speech-Language Pathologists (SLP). They work in coordinated synergy with your bedside nurse to accelerate functional independence and gait stability.

What happens during clinician shift changes?

We mandate a formal 15-minute bedside SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation) handoff between outgoing and incoming clinicians. Both review medication administration logs, patient vital trends, and specific family notes before the outgoing clinician departs.

Category II

Safety, Security & Vetting

How we protect your home, personal security, and verify caregiver integrity.

What background checks are performed on clinicians?

100% of employees undergo multi-state criminal history searches, FBI fingerprint identification, National Sex Offender Registry checks, Department of Health and Human Services (OIG) federal fraud screening, and 10-panel drug testing prior to hiring, with annual re-checks.

Are CareTrust clinicians bonded and insured?

Yes. All clinicians are direct W-2 employees of CareTrust (never independent contractors). We carry $5,000,000 professional clinical malpractice insurance, comprehensive commercial liability, and complete workers' compensation. Your family is never exposed to caregiver injury liability.

What if our matched caregiver isn't the right personality fit?

We offer a 100% Compatibility Guarantee. If for any reason the chemistry or personality match isn't ideal, simply call your care coordinator and we will introduce a new clinician immediately without awkwardness or disruption in service.

How do clinicians gain secure access to my home?

Depending on your family preference, we can utilize a secure Master Lock coded lockbox (installed at no charge), a smart lock integration, or have a family member present for entry. Lockbox codes are stored exclusively in our encrypted system and accessible only to assigned clinicians during scheduled shift windows.

How does CareTrust verify that caregivers actually arrive on time?

We utilize GPS geofenced Electronic Visit Verification (EVV). Clinicians can only clock in when their mobile device is physically detected within 100 feet of your front door. If an employee has not clocked in within 10 minutes of scheduled start time, our dispatch desk is alerted automatically.

Category III

Insurance & Financial Inquiries

Understanding claim submissions, out-of-pocket costs, and Medicare qualification.

Does regular Medicare pay for 24-hour round-the-clock home care?

Traditional Medicare Part A covers intermittent skilled care (e.g. 1-2 hour nurse visits, physical therapy sessions 2-3 times per week) for homebound patients, but does NOT cover continuous 24/7 custodial companion care. Continuous 24/7 care is typically funded via Long-Term Care Insurance (LTCI), Veterans Benefits, or private payment.

How do you handle Long-Term Care Insurance (LTCI) claims?

We have a specialized in-house LTCI Concierge Department. We coordinate the initial claim activation, provide physician certifications of ADL deficits, and submit weekly itemized invoices along with clinical shift notes directly to Genworth, MetLife, John Hancock, Mutual of Omaha, and all major carriers.

What is your cancellation or shift alteration policy?

We ask for a simple 24-hour phone notice to adjust, pause, or cancel a scheduled shift without charge. If a patient is suddenly admitted to the hospital in an emergency, all scheduled shifts are automatically frozen with zero cancellation fee.

Are home care expenses tax-deductible?

Yes! In many circumstances under IRS Publication 502, medical home health services and custodial care for chronically ill individuals certified by a licensed physician are tax-deductible medical expenses. In addition, payments can be made directly from your Health Savings Account (HSA) or Flexible Spending Account (FSA).

How frequently are invoices generated and what payment methods are accepted?

Invoices are generated weekly and delivered electronically through our secure Family Portal. We accept automated ACH bank transfer, all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), checks, and direct insurance wire transfer.
